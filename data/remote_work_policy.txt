Remote Work Policy — Sample Document

1. Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible for remote work up to three days per week, subject to manager approval. New employees must complete their first 90 days on-site.

2. Core hours
Employees must be available between 10:00 and 15:00 in their local timezone. Meetings outside core hours should be minimized and recorded when possible.

3. Equipment
The company provides a laptop, monitor, and headset. A one-time home office stipend of $500 is available after six months of employment. Internet costs are reimbursed up to $60 per month.

4. Security
All work must be done on the company-managed device with disk encryption and automatic screen lock after 5 minutes. Public Wi-Fi requires the corporate VPN.

5. Performance
Remote employees are evaluated on outcomes, not hours online. Quarterly goals are set with the manager and reviewed at the end of each quarter.

6. Expenses
Travel to the office more than 50 miles from home is reimbursed at the standard mileage rate. Overnight stays require pre-approval from finance.
